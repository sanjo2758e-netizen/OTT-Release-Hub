About OTT Release Hub

Last updated: August 2026

OTT Release Hub is an independent entertainment blog that helps viewers discover upcoming OTT movie and web-series releases across all major streaming platforms — Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar, SonyLIV, ZEE5 and Aha — in Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada and Hindi.

What we do

We publish original, easy-to-read articles about new and upcoming streaming releases. Each article includes the release date, platform, language, genre, cast, director, trailer (when available), a short synopsis and where to watch.

Our promise

  • Original content written by our team — no copied articles.
  • Clear release dates and platform information.
  • A clean, fast, mobile-friendly reading experience.
  • No intrusive popups or misleading ads.

Editorial note

All release information should be verified with the official platform before sharing. OTT schedules can change without notice. If you spot an error, please contact us.
