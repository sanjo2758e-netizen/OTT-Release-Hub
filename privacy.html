Last updated: August 2026

We do not set our own cookies. Third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdSense, if enabled) and analytics providers may use cookies to serve relevant ads and measure traffic. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Third-party advertising

If advertising is enabled, ads may be served by third-party networks. These networks may use cookies and device identifiers to show ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. If we add an analytics tool, it will collect aggregate usage data (pages visited, device type, country) to help us improve the site. We do not link this data to individuals.

This site is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
